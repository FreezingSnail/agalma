Agalma dashboard package

The independent agalma-web-dashboard process is installed and serving its compiled assets. Its live listener, hub-read binding and loopback URL are reported through the common plugin plugin.Health observation, not this static page.

Browser pages, registry navigation and refresh behavior are delivered by the later cockpit tasks; this page is the package-level health foundation.

Reads are observational. This process opens no ledger database, exposes no mutation route, and requests no factory control authority.
